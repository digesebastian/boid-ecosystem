
# Ecosystem of birds

Consisting of agents each controlling some sound. The agents compete with each other in order to take a bigger part in the soundscape. 

## Animals
### Starling 

Starlings  clump together and follow each other. Tries to group together into patterns which translate into chords.
For a starling flock the sound of the flock could be:
- The flock plays a single (diffuse) note according to the direction of the flock
- The flock plays a chord. The starlings each have a value they get initiated with. They create groups within the flock and each group placing itself at a certain position relative to the middle. This position determines the note played of each group of the flock.

Starlings bring more harmony
